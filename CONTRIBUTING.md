# Contributing

Suggestions, corrections, and substantive counterarguments are welcome. Open an issue or a pull request.

## What belongs here

Essays, forecasts, scenarios, research papers, and policy visions about AGI, superintelligence, and their consequences for society. Prefer substantial original arguments with a stable, publicly readable source. Include work that challenges the singularity premise as well as work that develops it.

Keep product announcements, tool directories, daily news, and generic AI tutorials out of the list unless they contain a substantial argument that fits this scope.

## Adding an entry

- Read the source and verify the title, author, and original publication year.
- Link to the author or original publisher. Remove tracking parameters. If the original is unavailable, label an archived copy or mirror clearly.
- Place the entry in the closest section, in publication-year order. Within the same year, alphabetical order by title is fine.
- Write one concise sentence summarizing the central argument in your own words. Attribute predictions and recommendations to their authors.
- Preserve the difference between a forecast, an illustrative scenario, and a policy proposal. Do not turn a conditional claim into a certainty.
- If correcting a date, attribution, or summary, link to the supporting passage in your issue or pull request.

Use this format:

```markdown
- **[Title](https://example.com/original)** — Author (Year). One sentence describing the argument.
```

For revised works, retain the original publication year and note a revision only when it materially changes the argument. Add a work once; choose the section that best fits its main contribution.

Contributions to this list are released under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE). Linked works retain their own licenses.
